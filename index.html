Welcome to SermonReach

SermonReach turns one sermon into a week of posts. You get a summary, six slides, and five video clips. You can also make three devotions and a discussion guide. Work from top to bottom: fill in Start Here, click Analyze Sermon, then use the five tabs.

Step 1: Start Here

Type the speaker's title, first name, last name, and the sermon date. Fields with a red star are required. Then pick YouTube Link or Upload Video File.

YouTube Sermons

Paste the YouTube link. Then click Paste transcript manually and paste the sermon's words. To get them, open the video on YouTube, click ...more, then Show transcript. Pasting gives the best results.

Uploaded Sermons

Type a sermon title and pick your video. It must be an MP4 or MOV file, 500 MB or smaller. Uploaded sermons are saved to the team's Sermon Library. Click Browse Sermon Library to reuse one without uploading again.

Step 2: Clip Focus and Analyze

Pick who the clips are for. Outreach is for people outside the faith. Discipleship is for believers who want to grow. Custom checks a question your community is asking. Then click Analyze Sermon. YouTube sermons take about 30 to 45 seconds. Uploads take longer.

Sermon Summary Tab

This tab holds a summary of about 500 words. Click Copy Summary or Download .txt. Read it before you post, and fix any names or verses that look wrong.

Sermon Notes Slides Tab

Click Generate Slide Images to download all six slides in one zip file. They are numbered in the order you post them. Each slide also has Copy Slide, Export Slide, and Download PNG.

Sermon Clips Tab

Each clip card has a caption and clip timing. Change the caption as needed, or click Regenerate Caption for a new one. Then click Create Clip. Clips take a few minutes. Wait for Clip ready.

Clip Timing

Click -5s or +5s to move the start or end by 5 seconds. You can also type a time, like 12:05. Each time can move up to 60 seconds from the AI suggestion. Click Preview to watch that part first. Click Reset to AI suggestion to undo.

Publish or Schedule

When a clip is ready, pick Instagram, Facebook, or YouTube Shorts. Click Publish Now to post it. Or click Schedule, pick a date and time, and click Confirm. A green badge means published. A gold badge means scheduled.

Devotions Tab

Click Generate Devotions to make three devotions, Day 1 to Day 3. Each has a title, a Scripture reference, a short reading of 250 to 400 words, one reflection question, and one action. You can edit every part. Click Regenerate on one day to get a new version of just that day.

Discussion Guide Tab

Click Generate Discussion Guide. You get the sermon's main points, Scripture references, discussion questions, one or two weekly applications, and a closing prayer. Edit anything you like.

Download as PDF

Click Download PDF on either tab. The PDF has the City Light logo and is set so it is harder to edit or copy. It is not perfect security, so share the PDF and keep your editable version in the app. Your edits save on their own.

History and Library

Open the menu at the top right and click Analysis History. It keeps your last 10 sermons. Click Load to bring one back. Published This Month shows every clip posted or scheduled this month.

Account and Team

Open the menu and click My Account to change your password. Click Sign Out when you are done. Admins can invite or remove people in Team Management.

Quick Tips
  • Create no more than 2 clips at a time.
  • Clips must be 0:30 to 3:00 long.
  • Over 1:30 still works, but Instagram shows it to fewer people.
  • Once a clip is made, its timing locks. Re-creating it uses an Opus credit.

Getting Around

Viewing Results
